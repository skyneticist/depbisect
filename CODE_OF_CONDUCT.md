# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct. The complete text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a welcoming experience for
everyone, and to act in ways that keep it open, friendly, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes showing empathy
and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes
and learning from them, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Conduct that the Contributor Covenant defines as unacceptable will not be
tolerated. See the linked full text for the complete standards and the
enforcement guidelines that maintainers follow.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at
**hunterhartline@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be
respected.
